BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few questions?

What does the free consultation include? +

An initial discussion of your project, its current stage and execution priorities. Site surveys, drawings and detailed quantities are scoped and agreed separately.

Can I request supervision only? +

Supervision, coordination or quality review can be discussed as separate services. Scope depends on project needs and availability.

How are cost and timeline determined? +

They depend on area, existing condition, material specifications and scope. Reviewing these details allows a tailored estimate rather than a generic price per square metre.

What should I prepare before getting in touch? +

Your project location, type, approximate area and current stage, plus any available drawings or photos to share during the conversation.
